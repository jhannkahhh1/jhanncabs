Good to know

A few quick answers.

Have a question about the estimate builder? Here is what this project website can—and cannot—do.

Are these the final prices?

No. The prices, finish options, rush surcharge, and bulk discounts are sample values for this school project. Confirm actual prices, materials, and availability with the business before accepting an order.

Does preparing a quote place an order?

No. The form only creates an on-page estimate preview. It does not send an email, submit an order, or save your contact information.

Does the artwork image get uploaded?

No. The image preview is created locally in your browser. The image is not sent to a server, and you can remove the preview with the button beside it.

Can I save or share my quote?

Use “Print / save as PDF” in the quote builder to save a copy from your browser. The quote itself stays in this page and is cleared when the page is refreshed.

How are bulk discounts calculated?

The sample quote applies 5% off for 100 or more items and 10% off for 500 or more items. Rush pricing is calculated after the bulk discount.
